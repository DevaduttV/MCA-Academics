Why Yenepoya Bangalore?

Established in 1991, Yenepoya University shared 3rd place among Indian universities in the Times Higher Education impact rankings from India. Yenepoya Bangalore has Well-furnished Air conditioned classrooms & Seminar Hall. Dual specialization degree programs with Advanced syllabus is available. Placement associated with Multinational Companies is one of the key highlights of Yenepoya Bangalore. Soft skill training is provided by industry experts.10000+ students have studied in Yenepoya. Yenepoya University is UGC Approved. And, holds many affiliations.

About Us

Yenepoya Bangalore Campus is one of the best Universities in Bangalore now. Yenepoya(Deemed-to-be University) was the first Deemed-to-be University in Dakshina Kannada. Yenepoya is now a well-known University in Mangaluru with multiple campuses inside and on the outskirts. Now We have started our new campus in Bhartiya City, Bengaluru. We provide the best-in-class education for our students and the best campus life. The facilities at Bhartiya City Include Bhartiya City Mall, Dynamic High Streets, BCIT- IT Hub, Contemporary Residential Complexes, Convention Centres, Entertainment hubs, Vibrant food streets, Smart Cafes & Restaurants, Coffee Shops and Restobars, Rooftop Cafes, Walkways and Urban cycle trails.
